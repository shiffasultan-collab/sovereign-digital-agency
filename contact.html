Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How quickly can you start? +
We can typically begin within 5-7 business days of signing. Our onboarding process includes a deep discovery session, competitor analysis, and strategy document — all completed within the first week. Active campaigns begin in week two.
What does the initial consultation include? +
Your complimentary strategy session includes a brand audit, competitor analysis, channel opportunity assessment, and a preliminary growth roadmap. It's a genuine strategy session, not a sales pitch. You'll leave with actionable insights regardless of whether you engage us.
How is MONARCH different from other marketing tools? +
MONARCH isn't a tool you can buy off the shelf. It's our proprietary AI platform built specifically for our workflow — integrating 29 platforms, running 24 AI agents simultaneously, and providing real-time optimisation that no manual team can match. It's the engine behind our results.
Do you require long-term contracts? +
No. We operate on rolling monthly agreements. We believe in earning your business every month through results, not locking you into contracts. That said, most clients stay with us for years because the growth compounds over time.
What industries do you specialise in? +
We have deep expertise across legal, property, health & wellness, finance, SaaS, e-commerce, education, and hospitality. Our team includes specialists who've worked in these verticals for over a decade, so we understand the nuances, regulations, and opportunities specific to each.
How do you report on results? +
You'll receive weekly performance snapshots and comprehensive monthly reports — not vanity metrics, but revenue impact, lead quality, conversion rates, and clear ROI. You also get access to a live MONARCH dashboard showing real-time campaign performance 24/7.
What if I'm not sure which services I need? +
That's exactly what the strategy session is for. We'll audit your current position, identify the highest-impact opportunities, and recommend a custom package. There's no pressure to commit — you'll leave with a clear picture of what's possible.
Can I start small and scale up? +
Absolutely. Many clients start with our Starter tier focusing on 1-2 services, see results, and then expand to Full-Stack Marketing within 3-6 months. Our month-to-month model makes it easy to adjust your engagement as you grow.
